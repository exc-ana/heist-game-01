# Midnight Vault — make.fun, 22 Nov 2026

A top-down stealth heist game: three vaults, three kinds of sentry, cameras
that feed an alarm meter, and a thief with coins, an EMP and a camera hack.

- **The Marble Hall** — a warden watches the diamond over the carpet runner.
- **The Bullion Cellar** — two strongrooms, two fast rovers, a safe to crack.
- **The Sky Vault** — an open rooftop, three lenses, diamonds out in the open.

Play it with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080. WASD moves,
SHIFT runs, C sneaks, click throws a coin, F fires the EMP, E hacks a nearby
camera, R restarts the vault. Carpet is silent, shadow hides, lamps light the
dark — and once the alarm hits 100%, lockdown hunts you down.
